શ્રીજી મહારાજની દૈનંદિની

Logbook of Shriji Maharaj, 1819–1829

Every Vachanamrut opens with the day it was spoken: the Samvat date, the village, the darbar, the time, where Maharaj sat and what he wore. Read together, the 273 discourses become a dated record of nearly ten years. Each date here has been converted to the calendar and checked against the others.

A day appears in this log only if a Vachanamrut was spoken on it. The other books in the library are not yet machine-readable text, so they are not part of this timeline.
